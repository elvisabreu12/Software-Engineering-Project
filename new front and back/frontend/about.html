About WHT_RBT

A helpdesk that gets IT problems to the right person, fast.

What it is

WHT_RBT is a helpdesk ticketing system. Employees report a problem as a ticket, IT staff pick it up, and everyone can follow the conversation until the issue is resolved. It keeps requests organised in one place instead of scattered across emails and messages, and it helps technicians find answers from problems that were already solved.

How it works

1

Submit a ticket

Describe the problem, choose a category and priority, and send it to IT.

2

It reaches the right technician

New tickets are matched to the agent whose skills fit the issue best.

3

Follow it to the fix

Chat with IT on the ticket and watch its status move from Open to Resolved.

What it can do

Ticket tracking

Create, search and follow tickets, with status and priority at a glance.

Conversations

Requesters and IT can talk on each ticket. Staff also keep private internal notes.

Roles and access

End Users, Agents and Administrators each see and do only what they need.

Skill-based assignment

Technicians list their skills, and tickets are matched to them automatically.

Helpdesk chatbot

An AI assistant answers questions using notes from tickets IT has already solved.

Personal settings

Edit your profile and password, and switch between light and dark mode.
