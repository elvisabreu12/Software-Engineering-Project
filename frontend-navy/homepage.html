Everything a help desk needs

Simple for the people asking for help, and clear for the people fixing things.

Quick ticket creation

Describe the problem, pick a category and priority, and send it to IT in seconds.

Track every status

See each ticket move from Open to In Progress to Resolved, with a full history.

Comments and notes

Requesters and IT talk on the ticket. Agents also keep internal notes that users never see.

Role-based access

End users, agents and administrators each see only what they need.

Admin overview

Administrators can review all tickets, manage the user list, and check login attempts.

Helpful assistant

Ask a question and get answers based on past solved tickets, with links to the source tickets.

How it works

Three steps from problem to solution.

1

Sign in

Create an account or log in. You land on the dashboard that matches your role.

2

Open a ticket

Fill in a short form. Similar solved tickets can help you before IT even replies.

3

Follow it to the fix

Watch the status, add comments, and get notified of progress until it is resolved.

An assistant that learns from every fix

When IT resolves a ticket and writes down what worked, that knowledge goes into the assistant. The next person with the same problem gets the answer right away.
